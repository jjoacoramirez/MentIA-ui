## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-energy digital training ground engineered for dynamic mental conditioning. Designed specifically for ambitious students and young professionals (ages 18–30), the experience steers strictly clear of sterile medical software, clinical lab dashboards, and juvenile game tropes like cartoon animal mascots. Instead, it positions cognitive agility alongside esports performance, flow state optimization, and interactive design tools.

The visual direction merges **Cyber-Minimalism** with an **Experimental Digital Sandbox**:
- Deep void-space canvases set a dark, distraction-free baseline that reduces cognitive fatigue.
- High-voltage cyan neural cues direct micro-focus, pulse during active gameplay loops, and provide sharp positive feedback.
- Expressive, geometric grotesque typography conveys technical mastery, speed, and modern digital craft.
- Tactile, pill-shaped interaction targets feel immediately responsive, physical, and tuned for rapid-fire thumb taps.

## Colors

The palette operates on high-contrast spectral accents over deep, light-absorbing surfaces:

- **Primary (`#06B6D4` - Cyber Cyan):** Represents flow state, neural synchronization, active selection, and hyper-focused interface states.
- **Secondary (`#6366F1` - Electric Indigo):** Delivers structural grounding, energy indicators, and deep cognitive training modes.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Amber Flare):** Reserved for XP gain, streaks, overdrive milestones, and motivational alerts.
- **Neutral Surface Hierarchy:**
  - `Canvas / Background (`#070A0F`): Deep space baseline.
  - `Surface Level 1 (`#0E1420`): Card decks and secondary modular trays.
  - `Surface Level 2 (`#162032`): Interactive game tiles, inputs, and active bottom sheets.
  - `Border Stroke (`#22324C`): Low-opacity structural boundaries.
  - `Text On Dark (High Emphasis - `#F1F5F9`): Sharp, uncompromised legibility.
  - `Text On Dark (Muted Emphasis - `#94A3B8`): Subtitles, metadata, and instructional hints.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Success / Match: Cyan `#06B6D4` & Bright Mint `#10B981`.
  - Error / Misstep: Crisp Coral `#EF4444`.

## Typography

The typographic pairing enforces a clear division of function:

- **Headlines & Metrics (`Space Grotesk`):** Infuses interactive cognitive drills with a technical, high-performance edge. Used for score counters, game mechanics, session timers, and drill titling. Its geometric, non-conformist letterforms anchor the digital sandbox persona.
- **Body & Continuous Copy (`DM Sans`):** Delivers clean horizontal flow and optimal readability for workout briefs, instant gameplay rules, and personal stats without visual friction.
- **Micro-Labels & Status Tags:** Set in `Space Grotesk` uppercase with tracked spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.04em–0.08em`) to mimic instrumentation readouts and arcade-inspired diagnostic panels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for fluid mobile-first ergonomic execution, adhering to a rapid 4px/8px incremental rhythm:

- **Mobile Viewport (Base):** Single-column layout with strict bottom-third thumb optimization. Screen padding anchors at `margin: 1.25rem` (20px) to prevent clipped taps near device edges.
- **Cognitive Arena Grid:** A responsive 4-column module inside drills with `gutter: 1rem` (16px), ensuring touch targets maintain minimum dimensions of 56px x 56px for twitch response training.
- **Tablet & Breakout Adapters:** Beyond 640px, the training matrix remains clamped at `max-width: 480px` for mobile simulation or expands into a split dashboard (drill field left, cognitive diagnostics right) with `gutter: 1.5rem`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a hybrid of **Tonal Layering** and **Neon Diffusion** rather than traditional drop shadows:

- **Base Layer (L0):** Solid, unlit background (`#070A0F`).
- **Mid-Tier Surfaces (L1):** Elevated container decks (`#0E1420`) with subtle 1px border highlights (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- **Active / Interactive Targets (L2):** Dynamic cards (`#162032`) framed with low-opacity cybernetic borders (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)`).
- **Glow & Radiant Elevation:**
  - Standard focus: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -4px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`
  - Overdrive / Streak: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`
- **Glassmorphism:** Bottom tab navigation and floating training heads-up displays (HUD) use frosted backdrops (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with `background: rgba(11, 15, 23, 0.75)` and a subtle top hairline border).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Level 3 (Pill-Shaped / Rounded Fluid)** geometry. This roundedness balances high-tech digital themes with approachable, organic human ergonomics:

- **Interactive Primary Buttons & Chips:** Full capsules (`border-radius: 9999px` or `rounded-xl` at 3rem) for a frictionless, ultra-smooth tactile response under quick thumb gestures.
- **Modules & Diagnostic Cards:** Bound by generous outer radii (`rounded-lg` at 2rem / 32px), creating fluid containers that separate game areas clearly.
- **Drill Interaction Tiles:** Softened capsules and rounded rectangular quadrants (`1rem` to `1.5rem`) eliminate visual harshness during high-speed eye tracking exercises.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Interaction Triggers
- **Primary Action (Play / Confirm):** Height: `56px` (`h-14`), full capsule radius (`rounded-xl` / 48px). Background in saturated Cyan (`#06B6D4`) with `#070A0F` bold Space Grotesk text. On active touch: subtle inward scale (`scale(0.97)`) with an outer cyan glow.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Translucent background (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`), 1.5px border in Indigo (`#6366F1`), text in `#F1F5F9`.
- **Game Answer Matrix:** Large tactile cards (height: `64px–80px`), background `#162032`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Selected state triggers instant cyan fill transitions with soft haptic audio feedback.

### 2. Chips & Filter Modules
- **Capsule Chips:** Height: `36px`, rounded pill geometry. Unselected: dark tint (`#0E1420`) with muted text. Active: primary cyan surface glow, contrasting dark label. Used for game difficulty, drill duration (e.g., "60s Blitz", "Zen Memory"), and category filters.

### 3. Cards & Play Arenas
- **Workout Drill Cards:** Double-layered surface containers (`#0E1420`) with 32px outer corner radius. Features embedded radial gradients at the top right corner indicating drill discipline (Cyan = Speed, Indigo = Logic, Amber = Focus).
- **Metric Cards:** Clean minimal layout highlighting numerical deltas, mini sparklines, and percentage gains with zero clutter.

### 4. Progress Bars & Timers
- **Neural Track:** High-velocity linear tracks with full pill caps (`height: 8px` to `12px`). Unfilled track in `#162032`. Active track uses a gradient from Indigo (`#6366F1`) to Cyan (`#06B6D4`).
- **Countdown HUD:** Dynamic radial timer with glowing cyan stroke and center Space Grotesk countdown timer.

### 5. Form Fields & Text Inputs
- **Search & Quick Input:** Height: `52px`, `rounded-full`, dark fill (`#0E1420`) with inset inner border. Focus state transforms the border to Electric Cyan accompanied by a subtle ambient outer blur. Placeholder copy is set in `DM Sans` muted slate (`#94A3B8`).

### 6. Bottom Navigation (4 Core Items)
- **Bar Styling:** Floating pill container docked to safe-area bottom, surfaced in frosted obsidian glass (`rgba(11, 15, 23, 0.85)` + 16px blur) with a hairline top border.
- **Items:**
  1. *Inicio* (Compass/Hub icon)
  2. *Entrenar* (Hexagon/Core pulse icon)
  3. *Progreso* (Activity sparkline icon)
  4. *Perfil* (User node icon)
- **Active State:** Cyan indicator dot underneath the icon paired with a soft icon glow; inactive states rest at muted opacity (`#64748B`).
